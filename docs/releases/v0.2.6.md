# v0.2.6 — 短时息屏与语音连续性排查

这是预发布测试版，语音仍可能缺帧、卡顿或中断，不作为语音稳定性已完成验收的版本。

## 下载与升级

- 首次安装或同时更新设备：下载 `Passport-Windows-x64-v0.2.6.zip`，完整解压后按包内「先读我-三步开始.md」操作。包含 `Passport.exe`、独立 USB 升级工具及配套固件。
- 只更新电脑端：下载 `Passport-Desktop-Windows-x64-v0.2.6.zip`。从程序或托盘菜单退出旧版后，完整解压新包，再打开 `Passport.exe`。无需重新安装 Python。
- 电脑端播放缓冲修复不要求刷机；设备的新增息屏选项、默认设置及诊断信息需要配套固件。已有保存的设置会保留。

## 本版更新

- 自动息屏新增 10 秒、30 秒选项；新设置默认 30 秒，默认亮度为 1，已保存的偏好继续保留。
- 电脑端等到足够填满一次音频输出并留出短暂余量后再播放，避免部分输出设备因回调较长而过早播放并夹入静音；结束录音时仍会送出尾音。
- 活动日志增加每段录音的缺帧、播放缓冲不足、音频驱动断流统计，便于反馈和排查；不保存录音内容。
- 配套固件增加只读连接诊断，供排查 Wi-Fi 语音停顿使用。

## 验证与限制

Python 与主机 C 测试、固件构建及 Windows 包的校验范围见[本版本验证记录](../validation/2026-11-11-v0.2.6-release.md)。单台设备、VB-CABLE 与讯飞路径中曾复现 5 秒设备回复超时；更换诊断固件并重启后，用户在未移动设备的情况下反馈后续录音未中断，但后台仍记录缺帧与缓冲不足。尚未确认根因或证明问题彻底解决。

第二台电脑、20 次重连、30 分钟稳定性、macOS、Typeless 和完整豆包实机验收未完成。语音默认关闭，需要用户自己的输入法及虚拟音频驱动；程序不包含 Codex、输入法或这些驱动。Windows 10/11 x64 需要 WebView2 Runtime，程序未代码签名。

## English

This is a prerelease for testing. Speech input can still lose frames, stutter or disconnect; this release does not establish reliable speech capture.

The all-in-one Windows archive includes the desktop client, USB updater and matching firmware. The separate client archive updates only the PC application: exit the old app from its window or tray, extract the new archive and open `Passport.exe`. The PC buffering fix works without reflashing; the new display settings and device diagnostics require matching firmware. Saved preferences are preserved.

New sleep choices are 10 and 30 seconds; defaults are 30 seconds and brightness level 1. PC playback now waits for a complete host audio block plus a short reserve, while retaining the final audio tail. Per-recording logs distinguish missing frames, playback buffer shortages and audio-driver underflows. The firmware adds read-only connection diagnostics; no recordings are stored.

One local VB-CABLE/Xunfei setup previously reproduced a five-second device reply timeout. After a diagnostic firmware update and restart, the user reported uninterrupted recordings without moving the device, but missing frames and playback shortages remained. The underlying cause and a complete fix are unconfirmed. Long-duration, repeated reconnect and second-PC acceptance are unfinished. The Windows x64 applications are unsigned; Codex, speech input methods and virtual audio drivers are not bundled.
